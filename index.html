How UPI Name Verification Works

1. Why doesn't any website fetch the bank name automatically?

NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) strictly limits direct banking name lookups to authorized, KYC-verified banks and payment gateways. Public websites are intentionally prevented from querying anyone's private bank account records by UPI ID alone to prevent stalking, data harvesting, and fraud.

2. How does the payer see your verified bank name?

When the payer scans this QR code in Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay or any bank app, their app queries NPCI in real-time and shows your official verified bank account holder name before allowing them to enter their UPI PIN.

3. What does entering your name here do?

Entering your name here embeds it inside the standard UPI specification parameter (&pn=Your+Name) and prints it neatly on your payment standee so the payer can easily verify who they are paying.
